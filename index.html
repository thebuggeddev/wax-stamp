Wax & DieTuesday evening, at the desk

Dear reader,

This page is a desk. The stamp in your hand has a brass die cut in reverse, so the design reads the right way round in the wax.

Press it on anything. This paragraph, the margin, the desk around the sheet. The wax pools, the die pushes it out into a raised rim, and the seal sets where you left it. Hold the press a little longer and the wax spreads wider.

Open the stamp in the toolbar to swap the walnut handle for cut crystal or fluted rosewood with a silk tassel, pick another die, or set your initials.

Yours,
The desk
